Open it on a Quest or a phone
  1. Phone and Mac on the same Wi-Fi. On the Mac: cd ~/strata && python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 0.0.0.0 (allow Python if macOS asks about incoming connections). Your Mac's address: ipconfig getifaddr en0
  2. On the phone, Chrome: open http://MAC-ADDRESS:8000/strata8-xr.html. The model, years and storm work straight away.
  3. Camera, tilt and AR/VR need a secure address: in the phone's Chrome open chrome://flags, search "insecure origins", enable it, type http://MAC-ADDRESS:8000, Relaunch. (Or with a USB cable: adb reverse tcp:8000 tcp:8000 and open http://localhost:8000/strata8-xr.html.)
  4. Real AR (the model sticks to your table) needs a phone on Google's ARCore list or a Meta Quest. Other phones get the camera backdrop: the model over the live camera, turned with your finger and the phone's tilt.
  5. Paste your Cesium ion token once on the device (it stays on that device).
  6. Quest: grip moves the city, right stick years, left stick size, A storm, B today, trigger puts it on a surface in AR. Phone: tap a surface to place it.
